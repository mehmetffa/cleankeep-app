Cleaner

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

This policy explains what information Cleaner ("the app") handles and why. Cleaner is developed by Mehmet Emin Yazıcıoğlu, an individual developer ("we", "us").

The short version

  • No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • Your photos and videos are analyzed on your iPhone. They are never uploaded, and we never receive them.
  • The app doesn't send any information to us or to third parties. Payments are handled by Apple.

Your photo library

Cleaner asks for access to your photo library so it can find duplicates, similar shots, screenshots, blurry photos and large videos. You can give access to all photos or only to the ones you choose, and change this at any time in the iOS Settings app → Privacy & Security → Photos.

All analysis happens on your iPhone using Apple's frameworks. Photos and videos never leave your device.

Nothing is deleted unless you choose it and confirm. iOS then asks you to confirm again, and deleted items stay in the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app for 30 days, where you can recover them. If you use iCloud Photos, deletions also apply to your other devices, as with any deletion in the Photos app.

Information that stays on your device

To avoid repeating work, the app stores the following only on your iPhone:

  • Scan results for each photo, such as a compact image fingerprint and sharpness score. These can't be turned back into your photos.
  • Your swipe decisions (keep or delete) and the category of each screenshot.
  • Settings and simple statistics, such as how much space you've freed and whether you finished the introduction.

Scan results, swipe decisions and screenshot categories are excluded from iCloud and computer backups. Deleting the app removes all of this information.

Screenshot sorting

On supported devices with Apple Intelligence, Cleaner can sort screenshots into categories such as receipts and chats. The text in a screenshot is read and categorized entirely on your iPhone by Apple's on-device model. The text itself isn't stored; only the category is kept, on your device.

Storage information

The app reads your device's total and available storage to show how much space is used and how much you can free. This stays on your device.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your name, e-mail, payment details or Apple Account. The app checks your purchases with Apple on your device to unlock Pro. Apple's handling of this information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, store or share personal information on any server.
  • We don't track you across apps or websites, and we don't show ads.
  • We don't use analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or other third-party SDKs.

Children

Cleaner is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because we don't receive or hold information about you, there's no personal data on our side to access, correct or delete. Everything the app keeps is on your iPhone and is removed when you delete the app. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Turkey's KVKK) you may have rights regarding personal data; contact us at the address below with any question.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
