Support

Cleaner helps you free up space on your iPhone by finding duplicates, similar shots, screenshots, blurry photos and large videos, all on your device. If you need help, the answers below cover the most common questions. Still stuck? E-mail us at scoutapp.support@gmail.com and we'll reply as soon as we can.

Using the app

How does Cleaner choose which photo to keep?

For exact copies it keeps the most complete one: a favorite first, then the highest resolution, then the oldest. For similar shots it keeps your favorite or edited one if there is one; otherwise the shot where faces look best (for example, eyes open), then the sharpest. You can always tap a photo to change what's deleted, or use Compare to see them full size.

Can I get deleted photos back?

Yes, for 30 days. Deleted items go to the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app, where you can recover them. After 30 days iOS removes them permanently.

I use iCloud Photos. What happens when I delete?

Deleting in Cleaner works like deleting in the Photos app: the item is removed from all devices that use the same iCloud Photos library and moves to Recently Deleted.

Why did the space shown in Settings not go down right away?

Deleted items still take up space while they're in Recently Deleted. To free it now, open Photos → Albums → Recently Deleted and delete them there. iOS may also take a few minutes to update its storage numbers.

How does Swipe work?

Swipe shows your photos month by month. Swipe right to keep, left to put a photo in the delete list. Nothing is deleted until you review the list and confirm. Your progress is saved, so you can stop and continue later.

How are screenshots sorted into receipts, chats and notes?

On iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, Cleaner reads the text in each screenshot and sorts it on your device. Sorting is part of Pro. On other devices, screenshots are grouped by month.

Permissions

Cleaner says it can't see my photos.

Open the iOS Settings app → Privacy & Security → Photos → Cleaner and choose Full Access. If you chose Limited Access, Cleaner can only check the photos you selected; you can add more from the app.

Subscriptions

What's free and what's in Pro?

Scanning and reviewing are free, so you can see everything Cleaner finds. Pro lets you delete what you select, delete everything you swiped away, and sort screenshots automatically.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open Cleaner → Settings → Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the iOS Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → Cleaner, then Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid being charged again. You keep Pro until the end of the period.

How do I ask for a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in and choose the purchase.
